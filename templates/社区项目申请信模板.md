# 社区项目申请信模板

使用前读取[申请策略分析](../modules/申请策略分析.md)。所有占位符必须来自真实资料；不适用内容直接删除。下列为待补全草稿，最终需提供语义一致的中文翻译。

**Subject:** Contributing to {{community project name}} in {{month}}

Hi {{host name or team name}},

I’m {{applicant name}}, and I’m interested in your work on {{specific community activity}}. {{A genuine reason this activity matters to the applicant.}}

{{A concrete example of relevant collaboration, organizing, practical, or creative experience.}} I could offer {{specific confirmed contribution}} and would like to understand how this could support the priorities your community has identified.

I’m available from {{start date}} to {{end date}} and could stay for {{length of stay}}.

{{One question about current needs, participation, or another important unknown.}}

Thank you for considering my interest. I’d appreciate the chance to learn more about the project and the role you have in mind.

Best,
{{applicant name}}

重点：尊重当地社区已有目标，不使用救世主式表述，不凭空承诺筹款、组织活动或改变社区。说明用户能持续参与多久。
