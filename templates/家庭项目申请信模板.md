# 家庭项目申请信模板

使用前读取[申请策略分析](../modules/申请策略分析.md)。所有占位符必须来自真实资料；不适用内容直接删除。下列为待补全草稿，最终需提供语义一致的中文翻译。

**Subject:** Helping with {{specific household tasks}} in {{month}}

Hi {{host name}},

I was interested in your description of {{specific detail from the listing}}. I’m {{applicant name}}, and I’d like to spend time with a family where I can contribute to daily life and learn about the local culture.

{{One or two sentences describing genuine experience relevant to the listed tasks.}} I could help with {{confirmed tasks within the applicant’s abilities}}, and I’d be interested to learn how you usually organize things at home.

I’m available from {{start date}} to {{end date}}, for {{length of stay}}. {{A genuine shared interest or cultural contribution, if relevant.}}

{{One concise question about availability or an important unclear condition.}}

Thanks for considering my message. I’d be happy to hear more about your plans for that period.

Best,
{{applicant name}}

重点：围绕日常生活和可靠性，不把「喜欢孩子／动物」转写为照护经验。涉及照护职责时依据真实资质和房东具体要求措辞。
