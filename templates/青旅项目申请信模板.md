# 青旅项目申请信模板

使用前读取[申请策略分析](../modules/申请策略分析.md)。所有占位符必须来自真实资料；不适用内容直接删除。下列为待补全草稿，最终需提供语义一致的中文翻译。

**Subject:** Joining your hostel team in {{month}} — {{applicant name}}

Hi {{host name or team name}},

Your description of {{specific hostel activity or atmosphere}} caught my attention. I’m {{applicant name}}, and I’m interested in helping with {{tasks mentioned in the listing}}.

{{A concrete example of genuine service, reception, teamwork, or relevant transferable experience.}} I could contribute through {{specific confirmed ability}}. {{Relevant language ability stated accurately, if provided.}}

I’m available from {{start date}} to {{end date}} and could stay for {{length of stay}}. {{A specific, honest reason for wanting to join this hostel.}}

{{One question about availability, shift arrangements, training, or another decisive unknown.}}

Thank you for your time. I’d be glad to discuss whether my experience fits what you need.

Best,
{{applicant name}}

重点：不要默认用户外向、英语流利、熟悉预订系统或能够独立夜班。无青旅经验时可以使用真实的服务、协作或组织经历。
