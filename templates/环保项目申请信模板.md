# 环保项目申请信模板

使用前读取[申请策略分析](../modules/申请策略分析.md)。所有占位符必须来自真实资料；不适用内容直接删除。下列为待补全草稿，最终需提供语义一致的中文翻译。

**Subject:** Helping with {{environmental activity}} during {{dates}}

Hi {{host name or team name}},

I’m {{applicant name}}. Your work on {{specific activity from the listing}} interested me because {{genuine personal reason}}.

{{Describe relevant practical, maintenance, recording, research, or teamwork experience without inflating expertise.}} I could help with {{confirmed suitable tasks}} and would like to learn more about {{specific area of interest}}.

I’m available from {{start date}} to {{end date}} and could stay for {{length of stay}}. {{Relevant confirmed fieldwork or living preference, if applicable.}}

{{One question about current tasks, training, accommodation, or another important unknown.}}

Thanks for considering my message. I’d be glad to hear what support would be useful during that period.

Best,
{{applicant name}}

重点：区分环保兴趣与专业知识，不虚构科学研究、野生动物处理或工具操作资格。不承诺未经验证的环境成效。
