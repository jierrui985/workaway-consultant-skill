# 教育项目申请信模板

使用前读取[申请策略分析](../modules/申请策略分析.md)。所有占位符必须来自真实资料；不适用内容直接删除。下列为待补全草稿，最终需提供语义一致的中文翻译。

**Subject:** Supporting {{learning activity}} in {{month}}

Hi {{host name or team name}},

I’m {{applicant name}}. I was interested in your description of {{specific learning activity or educational goal}} and would like to contribute to that work.

{{Describe genuine teaching, tutoring, mentoring, communication, or relevant transferable experience.}} I could support {{appropriate activity matched to confirmed skills}}. {{Describe the applicant’s actual language level or qualification only if relevant and provided.}}

I’m available from {{start date}} to {{end date}}, for {{length of stay}}. {{An honest reason for interest in this particular educational setting.}}

{{One question about the expected role, supervision, learner needs, or another decisive unknown.}}

Thank you for your time. I’d welcome a conversation about whether my background fits your needs.

Best,
{{applicant name}}

重点：不得虚构教师资格、母语身份或独立带班经验。区分辅助教学、语言交流和正式教师职责；涉及儿童的要求需按项目与当地规定核实。
