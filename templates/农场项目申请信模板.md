# 农场项目申请信模板

使用前读取[申请策略分析](../modules/申请策略分析.md)。所有占位符必须来自真实资料；不适用内容直接删除。下列为待补全草稿，最终需提供语义一致的中文翻译。

**Subject:** Helping with {{farm activity}} during {{dates}}

Hi {{host name}},

I’m {{applicant name}}. I was drawn to your project because of {{specific detail about the farm}} and would like to learn more about {{genuine interest}}.

{{An accurate account of relevant farming, gardening, outdoor, or hands-on experience.}} I could help with {{confirmed suitable tasks}}. {{If true, explain an unfamiliar task the applicant is willing to learn with guidance.}}

I’m available from {{start date}} to {{end date}}, for {{length of stay}}. {{A relevant work or living preference that fits the project, if confirmed.}}

{{One question about seasonal tasks, instruction, transport, or another important unknown.}}

Thanks for reading. I’d be happy to discuss how I could contribute during that time.

Best,
{{applicant name}}

重点：用实际经历说明活动强度适应能力，不写未经确认的「能吃苦」或「任何天气都能长时间劳动」。不将学习意愿写成机械操作、动物护理或专业维修资格。
