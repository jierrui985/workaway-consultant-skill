# Applicant match

## Check hard conditions first

Check overlapping dates, minimum stay, mandatory language or credentials, unacceptable tasks, living-condition limits, and any needed activity authorization. Mark each **Met**, **Confirmed conflict**, or **Unknown**. Unknown entry or activity authorization is not a finding of illegality, but it is not satisfied until checked.

You may describe a skills match despite a hard conflict, but the recommendation must reflect that conflict. A high score on preferences must not hide an unmet requirement.

## Weighted scale: 0–100

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence to consider |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Task skills and experience | 30 | Specific experience for core tasks and honestly described transferable skills |
| Dates and length of stay | 20 | Availability overlap, minimum stay, feasible arrival and departure |
| Language and communication | 15 | Ability to meet the actual communication demands |
| Working style and living fit | 15 | Intensity, teamwork, independence, accommodation preferences |
| Motivation and exchange goals | 10 | Interest in this project and alignment with personal goals |
| Reliability evidence | 10 | Previous sustained effort, completed commitments, or relevant examples; no automatic penalty for lacking a platform review |

Score each dimension from 0 to 5: **0** clear known conflict; **1** major gap; **2** partial fit needing substantial support; **3** adequate fit; **4** strong fit with specific evidence; **5** exceptional fit with substantial relevant evidence. Usually use whole numbers. Explain the host fact, applicant evidence, strengths, gaps, and an actionable improvement.

When all dimensions have evidence: `Match = Σ(weight × dimension score / 5)`, rounded to an integer. This is a decision aid, not an acceptance rate.

## Missing information

Mark a wholly unknown dimension **To confirm**; do not assign zero, full credit, or an arbitrary midpoint. Let `K` be the known weighted contributions and `W` the sum of their original weights. Then `Provisional match = 100 × K / W`; information coverage is `W%`. Provide one point value, not a range, and retain unknown facts in the report.

With no applicant data or `W = 0`, collect the minimum information before scoring. When enough is known for the main judgment, score provisionally, identify what might change it, and avoid repeated questions about minor gaps. Willingness is not experience. Keep internal precision, display the match as a whole number, and use the unrounded internal result for the overall score.

Example: If only skills are unknown and the known contributions total 56 with `W = 70`, the provisional match is 80/100 with 70% coverage. Do not claim the skills are suitable. Hard conditions and recommendation caps still apply. The report's opening line contains only the overall ten-point score; the match score belongs in the breakdown.
