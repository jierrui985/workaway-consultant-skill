# Illustrated report presentation

Open with exactly one line such as `Overall score: 6.0/10 | Consider with caution`. The next line gives the personal judgment and most important constraint. Follow [Overall assessment](overall-assessment.md) for evidence, missing-data treatment, and recommendation caps. Use clear numbering, short paragraphs, key amounts, and a few compact tables. Use cost callouts only where figures have a basis. If the interface has no cards, use Markdown headings and **bold amounts**. Do not repeat the same table pattern in every section.

Use these eight sections. A hard condition that changes the decision can be mentioned early and explained in its section. Do not invent numbers or media just to fill a layout.

## 01 Host basics

Give verified country, city/district-level precision and a map; project and people involved; any visible response rate or rating, pets, capacity, and activity date. Mark undisclosed fields **Not stated**. Do not guess a private address. One related image can appear with place and source.

## 02 Accommodation, meals, and actual costs

Separate lodging, meals actually provided, wages or allowances paid to the volunteer, and volunteer expenses. Present conflicting host and review accounts side by side, with dates and sources. A food allowance does not imply three provided meals. Only when prices, currency, and rates are sourced, show daily local-currency and preferred-currency costs and comparable 14-day and 30-day examples. Otherwise write **To confirm** and omit invented cost cards. Include meals on days off, room sharing, and extra charges.

## 03 Tasks and intensity

Describe each task, frequency, equipment, training, and physical demands. State **daily hours**, **working days per week**, commute, and physical intensity separately. If hours are absent, write **Not stated**. Do not misread “living with a student family 4–5 days per week” as “working 4–5 hours per day.” Task images must genuinely show the relevant work; omit them otherwise.

## 04 Past reviews and current activity

Give dates and sample size for reviews actually seen, and repeated positives and negatives. Date any visible recent activity or reply data. Older feedback describes the past, not this year's arrangements.

## 05 Location and nearby experiences

Show a map at the verified precision and day-off attractions with the conditions for reaching them. A relevant place image or direct link to a specific video may help; state author, source, and access date. Likes, views, and followers are distinct. If an image cannot be verified, link to its source or omit it. Never substitute an unrelated photo.

## 06 Door-to-door travel

Cover the applicant's **actual departure city** through the last leg to the project. For each segment give mode, date, original and preferred currency, and source. State unpriced legs, luggage, and night connections. A different month's advertised fare is not a quote for the trip dates. With only some priced legs, show a **known partial subtotal**. Follow [Travel costs](travel-costs.md).

## 07 Applicant match and application strategy

Check every explicit applicant non-negotiable and hard host requirement. Explain dimension scores so the weighted result can be recalculated. List 3–5 decision-changing questions in priority order and state whether the project is worth pursuing. A confirmed hard conflict outranks the score. Distinguish host claims, independent verification, inference, and unresolved details.

## 08 English host message and translation

Give a concise natural English subject and body, plus a consistent translation into the user's language when useful. Use only confirmed applicant facts for a real message. With a conflict over dates, credentials, or living conditions, write an inquiry first, without unconditional promises. For a fictional test, label the draft **Simulation only**.

## Images and privacy

Choose at most 1–3 images that genuinely help explain the project or confirmed place, with place, source, and link. Prefer official or clearly attributed images whose use is permitted. If the exact village is unverified, use a verified higher-level area. Do not display private homes or family photos without permission. If images cannot be shown, use titled source links. Never pass a generated image off as a photograph of the location.
