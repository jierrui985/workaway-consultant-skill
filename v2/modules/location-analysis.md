# Location analysis

## Locate and assess access

In the host overview, identify country, region/state, city or district (or the corresponding local unit), urban or rural setting, and the nearest major city in one or two sentences. Verify places with similar names and state the precision. Do not guess an exact location from photos. If a village is inferred only from reviews, label it **Inferred; confirm with host** and map the verified higher-level area. For a region-only location, use its center only as an approximate route starting point and note that the project may be farther away.

Provide a clickable Google Maps directions link to the verified city or district, not the host's residence. Use `https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=<URL-encoded city, region, country>`, with no prefilled origin. Label it **Google Maps: directions to [verified city/district]** and state that it does not locate the host's home. If only a region is known, navigate to that region and explain the limit. See the [Google Maps URLs documentation](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started).

Distinguish the geographically nearest airport from the most practical one by comparing routes, arrival times, onward transport, and the final leg. State major stations and verifiable travel times; do not invent distances.

## Judge the experience

Relate tourism, cultural contact, urban/rural living, public transport, groceries, internet, and access to essential medical care to the applicant's preferences. A nearby attraction is not automatically reachable on a workday; account for days off, season, and transport.

Living costs include uncovered meals, essentials, mobile data, and personal transport. Accommodation value includes privacy, sharing, location, and the applicant's needs, rather than a direct comparison to a hotel room price.

## Nearby places and specific media

Select one to three places that match the applicant's interests, season, and free time. Verify names, locations, and highlights with official tourism, park, or attraction sources where possible. Omit places with insufficient evidence rather than filling a quota. A compact **Place | Why it matters and how to reach it | Image or specific video** table is useful. State the starting city and transport mode. Do not present driving time as transit time. Label farther trips as **Day-off excursion** and leave unverified connections **To check**.

For selected places, look for a specific relevant Instagram post/Reel or YouTube video; one platform is enough. Prefer content directly depicting the place. Use a regional compilation only when the selected place is actually shown, with a timestamp when verifiable.

- Search with local and English names. Open candidate posts to verify subject, author, direct URL, and visible metrics. If the user sets a popularity threshold, apply it only after relevance is confirmed.
- Link to the individual post or video, not a profile, hashtag, search page, or playlist. List likes, views, and followers separately when visible; never substitute one for another.
- Verify likes on the actual content page or an accessible official interface. A search snippet can identify candidates but does not verify a current count. Views alone do not prove a post is highly liked. Do not ask for credentials or API keys just to collect metrics.
- If a platform requires login or hides likes, try the other platform without bypassing restrictions. If relevance is verified but likes are unavailable, keep the direct link and label **Like count not verified**. If no reliable content exists, omit it.
- Use social media to illustrate scenery or experience, not to verify fares, opening hours, safety, or project quality.

## Safety and information limits

Check official, time-relevant travel and weather or natural-hazard notices for the actual region. Explain their scope, dates, and sources without generalizing an entire country to a particular neighborhood. Do not promise absolute safety.

If current information is unavailable, state what was not checked and which point matters most. For entry status or permitted activities, refer to the relevant government's official information; a full visa assessment is outside this module.

## Output

Put a short, precision-labeled location and map in the host overview. Put nearby places, images, and videos in the location section. Avoid repeating the city introduction. Assess tourist appeal, everyday convenience, and practical access separately for overall scoring.
