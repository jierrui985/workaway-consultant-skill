# Overall assessment

## Six dimensions on a 0–10 scale

| Dimension | Weight | Assess |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Location appeal | 15% | Fit with interests, season, and living preferences |
| Travel cost | 15% | Door-to-door cost relative to budget, journey time, and feasibility |
| Tasks | 20% | Interests, clear duties, hours, training, and rest |
| Accommodation value | 15% | Food and lodging coverage, conditions, privacy, and needs |
| Cultural exchange value | 15% | Evidence of interaction and alignment with goals |
| Applicant match | 20% | The applicant match score divided by ten |

Score the first five in whole or half points. Anchors: **0–2** seriously unsuitable; **3–4** significant gaps; **5–6** workable with tradeoffs; **7–8** good fit; **9–10** excellent fit with strong evidence. Explain host facts, applicant preferences, and tradeoffs for each. Popularity or a low price alone does not earn a high score.

Without a budget, you may report sourced travel amounts but cannot score affordability. Without stated interests, do not substitute your own tourism preferences. These scores involve judgment. Use the default weights unless the user requests another set; weights must total 100%. Use the same weights when comparing projects.

With all dimensions scored: `Overall score = Σ(dimension score × weight as decimal)`. Round the final result to one decimal place and assign the default level from the **displayed** score.

Show one overall score in the report's first line, for example `Overall score: 6.0/10 | Consider with caution`. You may explain dimensions later, but never present a second overall score, optimistic/pessimistic totals, or a range. A single score is a judgment from current evidence, not a claim that unknown terms have been settled.

| Displayed score | Default recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| 8.5–10 | Strongly recommend |
| 7–below 8.5 | Recommend |
| 5–below 7 | Consider with caution |
| 0–below 5 | Do not recommend |

## Missing data and overriding conditions

Mark a wholly unknown dimension **To confirm** rather than scoring it zero, full, or at an arbitrary midpoint. Reweight only known dimensions: `Provisional score = Σ(known dimension score × original weight) / Σ(known original weights)`. Show a single one-decimal result, mark it **Provisional, based on known information**, and disclose the sum of known original weights as information coverage.

Calculate applicant match using [Applicant match](applicant-match.md), then divide by ten. If match is provisional, mark the overall score provisional too and disclose match coverage separately. Do not imply complete verification simply because every broad dimension has a number.

When evidence supports part of a dimension, you may score that supported part while listing unresolved critical conditions. With no scorable evidence or no applicant response to the intake, collect the minimum information instead of inventing a number. Do not repeatedly question minor gaps just to fill the opening format.

The following take precedence over the score:

- A confirmed hard requirement conflict, explicit applicant non-negotiable, or evidence-backed serious concern means **Do not recommend for now**. State what would have to change.
- Unknown available dates, key hours or meals, essential activity authorization, or major costs cap the recommendation at **Consider with caution**. State the decisive unknowns.
- Use **Strongly recommend** only when important conditions have been checked, no major obstacle is evident, and fit is well supported.

End with one or two sentences explaining who the project fits, its main advantage, and its main tradeoff or question. Do not present the score as an objective quality certification.
